**Last updated:** 2026-11-27. **Gates passed: 0 of 5.**

**What is authorized today.** A trial deployment on Azure is authorized and running (PO-20cf, PO-20cg). It runs on mock providers only, sign-in is invite-only Google sign-in, and the Product Owner approves every deploy. It clears no gate. Not authorized: real classroom recording or recording other people (G1); real speech or AI calls before the PO accepts the vendor terms in person (PO-20bl); open public sign-up; spending beyond the USD 25 a month budget; any clinical or effectiveness claim.
