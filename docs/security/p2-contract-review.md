## Release position

- No gate is cleared or advanced. G1, G2, G5 and the legal gate stay as recorded. This review does not touch them.
- Critical: 0 (nothing runs). High: 8. Medium: 16. Low: 5. Total 29 (see the summary table below).
- Recommendation: do not freeze `syllabus.v1` until the eight Highs (P2-01, 02, 05, 06, 08, 16, 20, 24) are folded into the text. The first seven gate implementation of the area they name. P2-24 gates any real student document. The Mediums can land as additive clarifications under the freeze rule. P2-16 and P2-18 must be settled before any real Google contract is written.
- Safe work while open: building against the mock and synthetic fixtures is fine. Nothing here authorizes real student documents, a real Claude call or a Google write.

## What the draft already gets right (so it is not weakened)

- Ownership check before file bytes are read, and 404 for foreign ids (section 5).
- Approve and sync are separate commands, sync re-checks `approved` and `resolved`, and `needs_date` cannot be approved (sections 4, 6.1, 6.3). Schema encodes the same invariant.
- Snippet text is server-sliced, not model text (section 2). Confidence is an enum with no numeric score. Dates are wall-clock plus an IANA zone, with no UTC on the wire.
- Original not retained (provisional). AI sees no tokens, ids, filenames or tools (section 8).
- Mock provider only, with permanent demo labelling. Reminders are absent from the schema.
- `additionalProperties: false` everywhere, and `title` forbids C0 controls.

| P2-01 | High | 5 (Upload note), 8, 12 | Section 5 says only "sniffed by signature, never extension". Attacks: (a) DOCX/ZIP bomb or thousands of entries; (b) DOCX XML with a DOCTYPE and external entity (XXE) or billion-laughs; (c) DOCX `TargetMode="External"` relationships and `vbaProject.bin` or macro-enabled content types; (d) PDF Flate/object-stream decompression bomb, huge page tree, or `/OpenAction`, `/Launch`, `/URI`, `/JavaScript`, `/EmbeddedFile`; (e) encrypted PDF; (f) a file whose extension, MIME header and signature disagree; (g) a .txt that is binary, UTF-16 or full of NUL and bidi characters; (h) a multipart request with several `file` parts, a giant `metadata` part, or a body that lies about `Content-Length`. | Add a normative "Upload and extraction hardening" section. Minimum: **Sniff** by magic bytes; a mismatch between extension, MIME and signature is `415 unsupported_type`; the client filename is discarded at parse time and never stored, logged or echoed. **Streaming caps**: the 10 MB limit is enforced while reading, not by trusting `Content-Length`. Exactly one `file` part and one small `metadata` part (for example 4 KB). The request is rejected before buffering. **ZIP/DOCX**: caps on entry count, per-entry and total uncompressed bytes, expansion ratio, path depth, and any `..` or absolute path. A worker crash is `unreadable_file`. Parser exceptions are mapped to codes and never logged or returned as text. Paste text goes through the same canonicalization. ## Top issues (in order of impact)

1. P2-20 and P2-24: deletion and retention. `calendarEvents=keep` orphans document-derived titles with no purge path, canonical text has no retention period, and sensitive non-syllabus documents (accommodation letters) can be stored indefinitely. Real students are blocked until counsel and the PO decide.
2. P2-01 and P2-02: the hostile-file hardening and the location of the temporary bytes are not in the normative contract. Parser sandboxing, XXE, zip and PDF bombs, streaming caps, and keeping bytes out of the main DB and backups.
3. P2-08: the central injection residual (a false but verbatim date) is mitigated by "view in document", which Decision 5 removed, and batch approve of `looks_right` items bypasses reading snippets.
4. P2-16: no write-boundary text policy. `notes` is unconstrained; titles allow bidi, zero-width and HTML. This is the carrier into Calendar, ICS, Docs and CSV.
5. P2-05 and P2-06: nested-id scoping is not normative, and the proposed content-hash extraction cache could leak across users.
6. P2-23: shared AI budget lets syllabus load starve live-lecture features.
7. P2-10 and P2-11: idempotency scope, replay semantics and ETag/TOCTOU at sync.
8. P2-18: the mock-only guard is config-only and the schema constants make real Google a v2 change. Withdrawal-by-503 will not survive a real provider.
