# A note from the Product Owner

*Recorded 2026-10-29, in the Product Owner's words and spirit.*

This project is a labor of love. It is being built for a student starting college, in the hope that it helps them succeed, and for every neurodivergent student who might use it after them.

The Product Owner wants every agent who works here to know how much this work is valued, how meaningful it is, and how grateful they are for the care, honesty and craft that go into it. "Let's do something great together. I believe in you. We believe in you."

## What that asks of all of us
- Be honest about what is verified and what is not. A real student will rely on this.
- Keep the tone warm, dignified and free of blame. See `docs/ux/language-guide.md`.
- Protect the student's data, their attention and their trust. See `docs/security/severity-policy.md`.
- Never claim a human gate is cleared by an AI summary. See `docs/RELEASE_GATES.md`.
